VESPER

Move a show between consoles without rebuilding it.

VESPER converts show files between Behringer X32, Midas M32, Behringer WING, Allen & Heath SQ, SQ+ and Avantis, and Yamaha RIVAGE PM, DM7, DM5 and DM3, with Allen & Heath dLive, DiGiCo, Avid S6L and SSL Live in development. Names, patch, processing, mixes and routing come across, and anything a console can’t do is named in the export notes, never guessed.
