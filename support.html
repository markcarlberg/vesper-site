Support

VESPER converts show files between Behringer X32/M32 and WING and Allen & Heath SQ, SQ+ and Avantis consoles.

Get help

The fastest way to reach us is from inside VESPER: choose Help > Report a Problem… It fills in the details we need (versions, consoles and any error) so we can help you faster. You’ll see everything before it’s sent, and your show file is attached only if you choose to.

You can also email markcarlberg@gmail.com. Please start the subject with “VESPER report” and include:

  • what you were converting (source console, destination console and firmware)
  • what you expected and what happened
  • the show file, if you’re comfortable sharing it

Before you load a converted show

  • Save the console’s current show first. Loading a converted show replaces what is on the desk.
  • Read the export notes. VESPER lists what it carried, what it approximated and what it couldn’t transfer. Phantom power (48V) is always listed first.
  • Check it before the show. Line-check and soundcheck a converted show as you would any new show file.

Common questions

Which consoles are supported?
Behringer X32 and M32 (scene files), Behringer WING (snapshots), and Allen & Heath SQ, SQ+ and Avantis (shows). More consoles are planned.

Something didn’t transfer.
Every console works differently, and some settings have no equivalent on the other desk. VESPER names each one in the export notes rather than guessing.

I opened one file of an SQ show and VESPER asked for the folder.
SQ shows are folders. Choose the show’s folder, and VESPER reads the whole show.

Privacy

VESPER works entirely on your Mac and never connects to the internet. See the Privacy Policy.
