Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · Mark Carlberg, doing business as MC Audio · markcarlberg@gmail.com

VESPER converts mixing-console show files on your Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version: VESPER does not collect, store or share your personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your show files stay on your Mac.

Your show files

VESPER reads and writes show files that you choose. All conversion happens on your Mac. Show files are never uploaded or sent anywhere by VESPER.

Support reports

If you choose Help > Report a Problem…, VESPER prepares an email to MC Audio. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself from your own email app or webmail, and you can read everything it contains before you send it. A report contains:

  • what you type in the “What happened?” box
  • VESPER’s version, your macOS version and your Mac’s processor type
  • the show file’s name and size, and the consoles and firmware involved
  • a summary of the conversion and any error messages
  • VESPER’s activity log from the last 7 days (file names, consoles and conversion results; no full file paths and no user names)
  • the show file itself, only if you tick “Attach the show”

Because the report is sent from your email account, MC Audio also receives your email address. We use reports only to answer you and to fix problems in VESPER. We don’t sell or share them, and we delete them when they’re no longer needed to support you.

Information stored on your Mac

VESPER keeps a few things locally, inside its own app folder on your Mac:

  • your preferences, such as the folders you last exported to
  • an activity log that is deleted automatically after 7 days

None of this leaves your Mac unless you attach it to a support report.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may provide MC Audio with anonymous crash reports. You can change this in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

VESPER is a professional audio tool and is not directed at children under 13.
