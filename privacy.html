Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-14 · Mark Carlberg, doing business as MC Audio · markcarlberg@gmail.com

VESPER converts mixing-console show files on your Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version: VESPER does not track you. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and your show files stay on your Mac. The only thing VESPER sends over the internet on its own is the optional “Stay in the loop” sign-up, and only after you press Join.

Your show files

VESPER reads and writes show files that you choose. All conversion happens on your Mac. Show files are never uploaded or sent anywhere by VESPER.

Support reports

If you choose Help > Report a Problem…, VESPER prepares an email to MC Audio. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself from your own email app or webmail, and you can read everything it contains before you send it. A report contains:

  • what you type in the “What happened?” box
  • VESPER’s version, your macOS version and your Mac’s processor type
  • the show file’s name and size, and the consoles and firmware involved
  • a summary of the conversion and any error messages
  • VESPER’s activity log from the last 7 days (file names, consoles and conversion results; no full file paths and no user names)
  • the show file itself, only if you tick “Attach the show”

Because the report is sent from your email account, MC Audio also receives your email address. We use reports only to answer you and to fix problems in VESPER. We don’t sell or share them, and we delete them when they’re no longer needed to support you.

Staying in the loop (optional)

VESPER can let you know about new consoles and fixes. The “Stay in the loop” window opens once, the first time you open VESPER after installing it. After an update, the What’s New window has a “Get update emails” button that opens it, and you can open it any time from Help > Stay in the Loop…. You don’t have to fill it in: Not now closes it, and VESPER works the same either way. Nothing is sent unless you press Join.

If you press Join, VESPER sends MC Audio:

  • your name and email address
  • what you do and the consoles you use, if you pick them
  • whether you ticked “Send me VESPER updates” (it starts unticked)
  • VESPER’s version, your Mac’s language and region setting, and the time you pressed Join

Nothing else: no show files, no device or advertising identifier, and nothing about how you use VESPER.

It goes to a Google Apps Script web app that adds it to a Google Sheet only MC Audio can open. Google receives your Mac’s internet address, as any web service does; we don’t record it. Google’s privacy policy covers how Google runs that service. If VESPER can’t reach it when you press Join, it keeps the sign-up on your Mac and sends it the next time you open VESPER.

We email VESPER updates only to people who ticked “Send me VESPER updates”, and every update email has an unsubscribe link that takes effect at once. If you didn’t tick the box, we won’t email you updates. We also use sign-ups to understand who uses VESPER and on which consoles. We never sell or share your information, and never use it for anyone else’s advertising. We keep it until you ask us to delete it.

To see, correct or delete what we hold, email markcarlberg@gmail.com and we’ll do it. Once you’ve unsubscribed, signing up again won’t put you back on the list; email us if you want back on.

Information stored on your Mac

VESPER keeps a few things locally, inside its own app folder on your Mac:

  • your preferences, such as the folders you last exported to
  • whether you’ve seen What’s New and the “Stay in the loop” window
  • a sign-up you’ve made that VESPER hasn’t sent yet (removed once it’s sent)
  • an activity log that is deleted automatically after 7 days

Apart from a sign-up waiting to be sent, none of this leaves your Mac unless you attach it to a support report.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may provide MC Audio with anonymous crash reports. You can change this in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

VESPER is a professional audio tool and is not directed at children under 13.
